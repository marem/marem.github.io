## Answer

- **Searches for "design strategy" at SA buyer level are very small.** None of the candidate terms had
  measurable volume in the sources available here. A phrase like "design strategy agency South Africa"
  most likely gets only tens of searches a month, or fewer. *(Unverified estimate; needs Keyword Planner
  or Ahrefs.)*
- **Directories own the results for every SA-qualified "agency" query.** For "design strategy agency
  South Africa", "strategic design consultancy Cape Town", "service design agency Johannesburg" and "brand
  strategy agency South Africa", nearly every result was a Clutch list page (clutch.co/za/...). DesignRush,
  The Manifest, TechBehemoths, Sortlist and Semrush's agency directory filled the rest.
  ([search evidence](#sources))
- **Directories win because of authority and page structure, not depth on the topic.** They have very
  strong domains and a separate page for each category, country and city (for example
  clutch.co/za/agencies/design/cape-town). Those pages exactly match "service + place" queries. Clutch
  does not have a "design strategy" category. Google maps the query to its "Design" and "Digital
  Strategy" pages instead, which list mostly web and branding shops. *(Interpretation of the observed
  results.)*
- **Nobody clearly owns "design strategy" as a category in SA search.** No SA firm's own site ranked for
  the core term in these results. That leaves an opening for a site whose pages are built around that
  exact phrase. *(Unverified for google.co.za.)*
- **Some adjacent terms surface real competitors.** For innovation and design thinking, the visible
  players are the d-school Afrika (UCT GSB), DesignThinkers Group/Academy SA, Motsoaledi & West, Specno,
  Deloitte and consultancy.co.za's innovation list. For service and human-centred design, they are
  …XYZ (dddxyz.com), How Might We, DVT Design and the Service Design Network SA chapter. For CX, they
  are Deloitte, The CX Group and individual CXPA-listed practitioners.
- **Spotted Ape and Marcel Maré have no visible presence in these results.** Branded searches for
  "Spotted Ape" agency, "thespottedape.com" and "Marcel Maré" design returned nothing related to either.
  Branded and category visibility both look close to zero. *(This is from a US index. Check it with
  Google Search Console.)*
- **A practical plan follows from this.** (1) Get listed on Clutch and DesignRush under "Design",
  "Digital Strategy" and "UX", with verified reviews, because those pages are what rank. (2) Build
  pages on your own site for the exact phrases, by city. (3) Get exact numbers from Keyword Planner with
  location set to South Africa, plus an Ahrefs or Semrush trial for difficulty and SERP ownership.

## Who ranks and why

Evidence comes from US-located WebSearch results for SA-qualified queries run on 2026-11-22. This is
directional, not a google.co.za SERP.

1. **Clutch (clutch.co/za/...) dominates.** It appeared for every "agency + SA/city" query tried: design,
   digital design, digital strategy, branding, UX, strategy development, and city pages for Cape Town and
   Johannesburg. Even a query that excluded "clutch" returned 19 Clutch pages. It has a large page set:
   one result reported 96 Cape Town and 145 Johannesburg firms in its digital-strategy categories.
   - *Why (interpretation):* each category and city combination gets its own indexable, paginated page,
     the domain is very strong, and reviews keep the content fresh. The top positions in Clutch lists
     are partly **sponsored**, so a paid placement on a page that ranks can be bought.
2. **DesignRush** ranked individual agency profiles (Sorted Design International, Stratitude, Modern Day
   Strategy, Envoque Creative) and city list pages. *Why (interpretation):* the profile pages target
   "agency name + service + city" and benefit from the domain's authority.
3. **The Manifest, TechBehemoths, Sortlist, the Semrush agency directory and GoodFirms** are a second
   tier of directories. They appeared for brand strategy, UX strategy, innovation and CX queries.
4. **Trade press and business directories** surface firms mentioned in articles: Bizcommunity (company
   pages and articles), ITWeb, IT-Online, SME South Africa, Fast Company SA. *Why:* these are SA news
   domains with strong authority, and appearing in an article or company profile there gives a firm both
   a ranking page and a backlink.
5. **Institutional and big-consultancy pages** rank for the innovation, CX and design-thinking terms:
   Deloitte ZA (Growth, Innovation & Ventures; Customer Transformation), UCT GSB / d-school Afrika, BSI,
   Accenture, TTEC.
6. **Agency Spotter** was barely visible. Only one SA-related project page surfaced, so it looks like a
   low-priority directory for SA.
7. **Specialist SA firms whose own sites surfaced:** dddxyz.com (…XYZ), dvt.co.za/services/dvt-design,
   designthinkersgroup.com / designthinkersacademy.com. Nothing was found that ranks for the exact phrase
   "design strategy". Backlink counts and domain ratings for these firms were **not obtained** because a
   paid tool is needed.

**thespottedape.com and marcelmare.com:** these could not be fetched (network policy), and they did not
appear for any category or branded query. Their status is **undetermined, and they are probably not
indexed for any category term**. Confirm with `site:thespottedape.com` on google.co.za and with Google
Search Console.

## What needs a paid (or account-gated) tool

| Need | Tool | How |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly SA volumes per term | Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) | Location = South Africa, language = English, paste the keyword table. Accounts with low or no spend see ranges such as 0, 1–100 or 100–1K instead of exact numbers ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-officially-throttling-keyword-planner-data-low-spending-adwords-accounts-255795), [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-keyword-planner-data-ranges-campaign-only-22506.html), [Dyno Mapper 2026](https://dynomapper.com/blog/search-engine-optimization/how-to-use-googles-keyword-planner-like-a-pro/)). A small, short campaign usually unlocks exact figures (practitioner reports, unverified). The forecast tool still works without spend ([SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-keyword-planner-forecasting-258053)). |
| Keyword difficulty, SERP ownership and backlinks of rankers | Ahrefs or Semrush (paid; trials or limited free tiers) | Set the database to ZA. Run Keywords Explorer on the table and check SERP overview for the top 10 per term. Run Site Explorer on clutch.co/za/agencies/design/cape-town, dddxyz.com and dvt.co.za to see referring domains. |
| Where Spotted Ape and marcelmare.com rank today | Google Search Console (free) | Verify both domains. The Performance report filtered to country = South Africa shows real queries, impressions and average position. This is the most reliable source for a small site. |
| Relative interest over time | Google Trends (free) | Geo = South Africa. Compare "service design", "design thinking", "customer experience" and "design strategy". It gives relative interest only, not volumes. |
| Actual google.co.za SERP order | A manual check or a SERP API | Search from SA in an incognito window, or use a rank tracker set to google.co.za. |
