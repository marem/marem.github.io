# Spotted Ape

The language used when planning how Spotted Ape becomes the leading design strategy agency in South Africa.

## Language

**Spotted Ape**:
The design strategy agency trading at thespottedape.com.
_Avoid_: The Spotted Ape (as a brand name), Spooted Ape, thespootedape.com

**Design strategy agency**:
The market category Spotted Ape competes to lead: a firm that sells strategy shaped by design practice. The category buyers already search for and recognise.
_Avoid_: Strategic Execution Engine, strategic design consultancy (as the category name)

**No. 1**:
The combination of top organic search ranking in South Africa for the category's search terms and first place in target buyers' unprompted recall of the category.
_Avoid_: Biggest, best, award-winning (as the definition of leading)
