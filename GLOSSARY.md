# Spotted Ape

The language used when planning how Spotted Ape becomes the leading design strategy agency in South Africa.

## Language

**Spotted Ape**:
The design strategy agency trading at thespottedape.com.
_Avoid_: The Spotted Ape (as a brand name), Spooted Ape, thespootedape.com

**Design strategy agency**:
The market category Spotted Ape competes to lead: a firm that sells strategy shaped by design practice. The category buyers already search for and recognise.
_Avoid_: Strategic Execution Engine, strategic design consultancy (as the category name)

**No. 1**:
The combination of top organic search ranking in South Africa for the category's search terms and first place in target buyers' unprompted recall of the category.
_Avoid_: Biggest, best, award-winning (as the definition of leading)

**Founder site**:
marcelmare.com, the personal authority site of Spotted Ape's founder, which feeds credibility into Spotted Ape rather than competing with it.
_Avoid_: Agency site, portfolio (when meaning marcelmare.com)

**Target buyer**:
The person who commissions design strategy: primarily heads of marketing, CX, innovation or strategy at large South African corporates; secondarily founders and CEOs of scale-ups.
_Avoid_: Client (before they have bought), customer, audience

**Agentic Mesh**:
Spotted Ape's network of AI agents that executes strategy at machine speed under human steering; the primary differentiator within the design strategy agency category.
_Avoid_: AI tools, automation, bots

**Human-in-the-loop (HITL)**:
The human steering layer that frames problems and governs the Agentic Mesh.
_Avoid_: Oversight, human review
